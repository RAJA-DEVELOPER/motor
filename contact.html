For standard cases, we issue motor insurance policies on the same day. Once your documents are verified and premium is paid, you'll receive your policy certificate within 30 minutes via email. For comprehensive policies with inspection requirements, it may take up to 24 hours.
For RC transfer, you need: Original RC, Form 29 & 30 signed by the seller, insurance in the buyer's name, valid PUC, address and ID proof of the buyer, and road tax receipt. For inter-state transfers, you'll also need an NOC from the original state's RTO.

Yes, you can renew an expired policy. However, if your policy has been lapsed for more than 90 days, you may lose your No-Claim Bonus and the insurer may require a vehicle inspection before issuing the renewal. We recommend renewing before expiry to avoid these issues.
Under the Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act, the penalty for not having a valid PUC certificate is ₹10,000 for the first offence. For subsequent offences, the penalty can go up to ₹10,000 or imprisonment up to 6 months, or both. A valid PUC is also mandatory for insurance claims.
