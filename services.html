Detailed Overview

Services We Offer

Get comprehensive or third-party motor insurance for two-wheelers and four-wheelers. We compare plans from 50+ insurers to find the best coverage at the most competitive premium.

Coverage includes: Own damage, theft, fire, natural calamity, third-party liability, personal accident, and optional add-ons like zero depreciation, roadside assistance, and engine protection.

Required documents: Vehicle RC, previous insurance (if any), PAN card, Aadhaar card, and vehicle inspection photos.

Processing time: Same-day policy issuance for standard cases.

Seamless policy renewal with no-claim bonus protection. We send timely reminders before your policy expires and help you choose between your existing insurer or switch to a better plan.

Benefits: No-claim bonus preservation, premium comparison across providers, lapse period coverage advisory, and instant renewal certificate.

Required documents: Existing policy copy, vehicle RC, and valid ID proof.

Processing time: Same-day issuance.

Complete end-to-end RC transfer service for vehicle ownership changes. We handle RTO form filing, fee payment, inspection coordination, and follow-ups for both within-state and inter-state transfers.

Process includes: Form 29/30 preparation, NOC procurement (for inter-state), RTO submission, inspection scheduling, and new RC delivery.

Required documents: Original RC, insurance in buyer's name, seller's Form 29/30, address proof of buyer, and valid ID of both parties.

Processing time: Within-state: 15–30 days. Inter-state: 30–60 days.

Mandatory fitness certificate procurement and renewal for transport and commercial vehicles. We manage vehicle inspection scheduling, form filing, and certificate delivery.

Applicable for: Commercial vehicles, taxis, buses, trucks, and vehicles older than 15 years (as per new regulations).

Required documents: Vehicle RC, insurance certificate, PUC certificate, previous fitness certificate (if renewal), and tax payment receipt.

Processing time: 7–15 working days.

Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate assistance including nearest center location, validity tracking, and renewal reminders. Avoid fines with timely PUC compliance.

Penalties for non-compliance: ₹10,000 fine for first offence under Motor Vehicles Act. We help you stay compliant at all times.

Required: Vehicle RC and existing PUC (if renewal). No appointment needed at most centers.

Validity: 6 months for new vehicles, 1 year for older vehicles.
